---

## 🎯 Vision & Philosophy

Most modern finance apps require sending your personal spending, bank SMS, and sensitive receipts to remote cloud servers or third-party LLM providers. 

**Expense Tracker is built differently:**
* 🔒 **100% Privacy-First:** Your financial records and transaction history never leave your device.
* ⚡ **Instant & Offline-First:** Operates flawlessly in airplane mode and zero-signal environments.
* 🧠 **Hybrid On-Device AI:** Combines zero-latency heuristic parsing with an optional on-demand Small Language Model (SLM) for natural-language expense logging and receipt analysis — completely on-device, with zero recurring token fees.
* 📊 **Smart Wealth Tracking:** Habit-building streaks, visual category breakdowns, and wealth trajectory analytics.
